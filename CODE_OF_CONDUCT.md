# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
Ludo a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient — most people here are doing this in their spare time.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit messages — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, via the contact details on
their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Covin90) or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Covin90/ludo/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather it not be public. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when
they do.
